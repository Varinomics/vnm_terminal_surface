# Contributing

Contributions to this repository follow the
[Varinomics contribution policy](docs/contributions/contribution-policy.md).
It preserves contributors' ownership while allowing Varinomics to license
their work under GPL and alternative terms, including proprietary terms and
downstream sublicensing. The repository's [GPL licence](LICENSE) remains in
effect for its GPL distribution.

Each human author and co-author needs a verified
[individual CLA](docs/contributions/individual-cla-v1.0.md). Work controlled
by an employer or client also needs the relevant
[corporate grant and authorisation](docs/contributions/corporate-cla-v1.0.md).
Send the completed agreement privately to **info@varinomics.com** as its form
instructs; read the [contribution privacy notice](docs/contributions/privacy.md).

Use the pull-request template to identify every author and disclose imported
or adapted material. A CLA does not approve third-party code or assets:
maintainers separately review the actual licence and keep origins and
obligations in [THIRD_PARTY/](THIRD_PARTY/) and
[third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), following the
[import procedure](docs/contributions/third-party-materials.md).

Both `cla/coverage (records N)` and
`contributions/material-review (records N)` must pass before merge, with `N`
matching the registry generation required by the repository's merge rules.
See [agreement administration](docs/contributions/administration.md)
for identity corrections, corporate rosters, versioned records and narrowly
scoped exceptions. Existing commits are not automatically cleared for
alternative licensing by the adoption of this policy.
