# Contributing

Open your pull request normally. The CLA bot links the agreement and shows
how to accept it with a comment from your own GitHub account. Acceptance is
recorded automatically and reused for future pull requests to this repository
under that agreement version and capacity. No emailed form or upload is needed.

Choose the [individual CLA v1.1](docs/contributions/individual-cla-v1.1.md) for
rights you control, or the
[corporate CLA v1.1](docs/contributions/corporate-cla-v1.1.md) if you can bind
the named company or other rights holder. Corporate acceptance covers your
own and the Entity's relevant rights without a second individual signature.
An authorised representative can use the online procedure for other authors
whose necessary rights they control.

Contributors retain ownership while allowing GPL and alternative licensing,
including proprietary terms and downstream sublicensing. The repository's
[GPL licence](LICENSE) remains applicable to its GPL distribution. Read the
[contribution policy](docs/contributions/contribution-policy.md) and
[privacy notice](docs/contributions/privacy.md).

Identify all authors and disclose imported or adapted material in the
pull-request template. A CLA does not approve an import: maintainers inspect
the actual licence and keep origins and obligations in [THIRD_PARTY/](THIRD_PARTY/)
and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), using the
[import procedure](docs/contributions/third-party-materials.md).
