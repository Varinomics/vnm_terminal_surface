Agreement identifier: **Varinomics-CCLA-1.0**

Version: **1.0**

Published: **2026-11-06**

This agreement is between the legal entity identified in the execution
record (the "Entity") and **Varinomics Ltd**, a company incorporated in Cyprus
under registration number **HE 434980**, with registered office at **Agiou
Athanasiou 59, 4102 Limassol, Cyprus** ("Varinomics"). The signatory acts for
the Entity. Read the whole agreement before signing and retain the completed
agreement and schedules.

## 1. Contributions, scope and ownership

A "Project" is a Varinomics repository that expressly adopts this agreement
in its contribution policy. A "Contribution" is original code, documentation,
artwork or other material intentionally submitted to Varinomics for inclusion
in a Project by a contributor whom the Entity authorises in Schedule A,
within the scope of that authorisation, and in which the Entity owns or
controls the rights granted here. This includes original changes to existing
material. Material clearly marked "Not a Contribution" is excluded.

This agreement covers Contributions submitted on or after execution while
the relevant contributor's authorisation is effective. Earlier Contributions
are covered only when specifically identified in Schedule B. The grant for
earlier material begins on execution, not on an invented earlier signing date.

**The Entity retains ownership of its Contributions. This agreement grants
a nonexclusive licence and does not assign copyright.** It does not grant
rights belonging to an employee, contractor, affiliate or another person
that the Entity lacks authority to license. An affiliate is covered only
when expressly identified in the execution record and the signatory has
authority to bind it.

## 2. Copyright and downstream rights

For rights the Entity owns or is authorised to grant in each Contribution,
the Entity grants Varinomics a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, no-charge,
royalty-free, irrevocable and transferable licence to use, reproduce, modify,
adapt, translate, create derivative works of, publicly display, publicly
perform, make available and distribute the Contribution and derivative
works, in source, object and other forms, alone or with other material.

The grant expressly includes licensing and sublicensing under the GNU
General Public License and alternative terms, including other free or
open-source licences, commercial licences and proprietary licences.
Varinomics may authorise its licensees and sublicensees, and their successors
and downstream recipients, to exercise and further sublicense those rights
through multiple successive tiers without further permission or payment to
the Entity.

To the extent the Entity can lawfully provide it, the grant includes consent
to the modification, combination, publication and distribution contemplated
here and nonassertion of moral or similar rights to prevent those acts.
It does not transfer inalienable personal rights or permit omission of
attribution or notices required by law or a controlling third-party licence.
The Entity must identify any necessary consent that it cannot itself grant.

## 3. Patent rights

The Entity grants Varinomics and recipients authorised through the licences
in section 2 a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, no-charge, royalty-free,
irrevocable and transferable patent licence, including rights to sublicense
through successive tiers, to make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell,
import and otherwise transfer each Contribution and its combination with the
Project to which it is submitted. The grant covers only claims the Entity
owns or controls and can license that are necessarily infringed by that
Contribution alone or by that combination. It does not extend to unrelated
inventions or rights the Entity cannot license.

## 4. Corporate authority and disclosures

The Entity confirms that it can enter into this agreement, grant its rights
and authorise the designated contributors. The person signing confirms that
they are authorised to bind the Entity. Except for separately disclosed
third-party material, Contributions covered by the grants are original work
whose relevant rights the Entity owns or controls, and these grants do not
conflict with its obligations to another person.

Schedule A identifies each authorised contributor, the Projects and work
covered, and the effective dates. Changes must be sent in writing by a
verified authorised representative. Removing a contributor or narrowing
authorisation affects future submissions only and does not revoke grants
already validly made. Organisation membership, an email domain or a person's
individual CLA alone does not establish the Entity's authorisation.

Imported or adapted material must be separately identified with its source,
authors or rights holders, actual licence and known restrictions. Nothing in
this agreement grants another person's rights or approves an import. The
Entity must disclose rights retained by individual authors, affiliates,
clients or other persons so the required separate permission can be obtained.
It must notify Varinomics when it learns that its authority, schedule or
rights disclosures are materially inaccurate or have changed.

## 5. Limits and continuing effect

No trademark rights are granted. Varinomics need not accept or publish a
Contribution. The Entity and its designated contributors have no obligation
to maintain Contributions, provide assistance or make further submissions.
Except for the express authority and disclosure statements and obligations
that law does not permit excluding, Contributions are provided as they are,
without implied warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular
purpose or noninfringement. This agreement imposes no indemnity obligation.

The Entity may stop authorising new Contributions. Grants already validly
made survive that decision, employment changes, account closure and the end
of participation. Transfers of Varinomics' rights must preserve its
applicable obligations and the reservations of the Entity's rights. This
agreement does not change a Project's published licence or restrict rights
already granted to recipients.

## 6. Agreement and execution

The laws of the Republic of Cyprus govern this agreement, excluding their
conflict-of-laws rules, and disputes are subject to the courts of Cyprus,
except where mandatory law provides otherwise. Nothing excludes a right or
liability that applicable law does not permit excluding. An unenforceable
provision does not invalidate remaining provisions that can operate lawfully.

This agreement and the completed execution record and schedules state the
corporate contribution grant. Amendments require express written agreement;
a newly published version does not amend a signed version. Electronic
execution is permitted. The exact agreement accepted must be retained with
its execution record. An automated check does not establish corporate
authority or create rights that were not granted.
