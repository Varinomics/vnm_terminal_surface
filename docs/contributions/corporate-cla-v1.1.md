## 1. Contributions, scope and ownership

A "Project" is the Varinomics repository in which you accept this agreement
through its CLA bot. A "Contribution" is original code, documentation,
artwork or other material intentionally submitted to Varinomics for inclusion
in that Project by you or an additional contributor expressly authorised in
an online record by an Entity representative. Each Contribution must be within
the scope of that authorisation. The grants cover the rights that you and the
Entity respectively own or control, including your original changes to
existing material. Material clearly marked "Not a Contribution" is excluded.

This agreement covers original Contributions intentionally submitted to this
Project before or after acceptance in the stated corporate capacity, but
only rights you and the named Entity own or are authorised to grant. For an
additional contributor the work must be within the representative's online
authorisation and the Entity's actual rights. Grants begin on actual
acceptance, not an invented earlier signing date; another person's historic
rights are not granted by this agreement. Future corporate Contributions
must remain within the relevant corporate authority. This acceptance does
not sign for another repository or a different employer on your behalf.

**The Entity retains ownership of its Contributions. This agreement grants
a nonexclusive licence and does not assign copyright.** It does not grant
rights belonging to an employee, contractor, affiliate or another person
that the Entity lacks authority to license. You retain ownership of material
you personally own and license here. An affiliate is covered only if expressly
named and you have authority to bind it.

### Online corporate acceptance

Reply from your own GitHub account with the corporate acceptance command
shown by the CLA bot and the Entity's legal name. That command expressly
accepts **Varinomics-CCLA-1.1** for you and the Entity, grants the rights each
controls, and confirms your authority to bind the Entity. Do not use this
route unless that representation is true. An authorised representative may
use the bot's online authorisation procedure to cover additional named
contributors and work for which the representative has the necessary rights.

Use your actual Entity and authority details in this command:

```text
/cla agree corporate v1.1 {"company":"Exact legal entity","role":"Position or signing authority"}
```

To cover additional contributors when you control the necessary rights,
include `"authors":["@alice","@bob"]` in the JSON object. After corporate
acceptance, its representative can add covered contributors with:

```text
/cla authorize v1.1 {"company":"Exact legal entity","authors":["@alice"]}
```

The bot resolves the named GitHub accounts to numeric identities. These
commands identify the relevant work as original Contributions submitted by
those authors to this Project in the named corporate capacity before or
after acceptance, limited to rights and authority the representative actually
controls.

The bot automatically retains the exact assent, company name and stated role
or signing authority, agreement
version and digest, accepted text, authenticated numeric actor identity,
source comment event and time. Future pull requests to the same repository
in the covered capacity reuse this record. No emailed agreement, document
upload or routine manual registration is required. The original comment
is not the sole archive; editing or deleting it does not erase the retained
record or revoke a valid grant.

The company name and acceptance are public in a public pull request;
private-repository access follows its permissions. Do not post residential
addresses, identity documents or unnecessary private information. Further
authority verification may be needed for an actual ambiguity or dispute,
but is not a routine prerequisite. This is recorded electronic assent, not
a claim of a qualified electronic signature or conclusive authority proof.
See [administration](administration.md) and [privacy](privacy.md).
